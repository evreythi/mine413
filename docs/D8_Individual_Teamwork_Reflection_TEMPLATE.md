# Part A - Individual reflection (suggested 600-900 words)

Use specific examples (dates, decisions from the decision log, pull requests, meeting minutes). Avoid generalities.

1. **My contribution.** What did I own (work package, files/modules, sections of D2-D7)? What did I review for others? Give evidence (commit IDs, run IDs, decision-log entries).
2. **Coordination.** How did my work depend on others' (e.g. QA/QC decisions → algorithm inputs → block model)? Describe one hand-over that went well and one that did not, and why.
3. **Leadership and followership.** Describe a moment I led (chaired, proposed a method, unblocked someone) and a moment I deliberately followed or supported another member's lead.
4. **Conflict or disagreement.** Describe one technical or process disagreement (for example survey timing, density basis, how to present legacy material, whether to impute grades). How was it resolved under the charter? What would I do differently?
5. **Workload equity.** Was the work shared fairly? If not, what was done about it, and when? Cite the contribution record.
6. **Inclusive practice.** What did I do to make sure everyone was heard (quieter members, different schedules, different levels of coding experience)?
7. **Use of AI tools.** What AI assistance did *I* use, for what, and how did *I* verify it? (This must be consistent with the team disclosure in D6 Appendix A.)
8. **Learning.** One technical lesson and one teamwork lesson I will carry into professional practice.
